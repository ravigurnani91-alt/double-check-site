CASE 0010

Augustinus Bader The Cream Review: What Is TFC8, Really?

Moisturizers · Luxury · 7 min read
MIXED

The Claim

TFC8® (Trigger Factor Complex) — backed by "over 35 years of research" — improves skin cell communication, creating the optimal environment for continuous renewal and visible rejuvenation.

What's Actually In It (Key Ingredients from a 52-Ingredient List)

IngredientVerdictEvidence
Palmitoyl Tripeptide-8VERIFIEDA studied peptide associated with skin conditioning and collagen-supportive signaling.
Ceramide NP, Ceramide NGVERIFIEDCore skin-barrier lipids with a strong, well-established evidence base.
Alanyl Glutamine, ArginineMIXEDAmino acids with humectant and some wound-healing research support, but evidence specifically for anti-aging/firmness claims at cosmetic concentrations is limited.
Retinyl PalmitateMIXEDA gentler, less-studied ester form of vitamin A compared to retinol — plausible but weaker evidence for visible anti-aging effects than direct retinol or retinoic acid.
"TFC8®" (Trigger Factor Complex)MIXEDNot a single disclosed INCI ingredient — it's the brand's proprietary name layered over combinations of the vitamins, lipids, and peptides above. Exact ratios and unique mechanism are not independently verifiable. The brand's own cited trials are small: a 4-week study with 20 participants for anti-wrinkle claims, and a 4-week perception study with 52 participants.

Case File Verdict

The individually disclosed actives — peptides, ceramides, amino acids — are genuinely well-chosen and evidenced for hydration and barrier support. But "TFC8" as a headline technology is a proprietary complex, not an independently verifiable ingredient, and the brand's own supporting studies are small in scale (20-52 participants over 4 weeks) relative to the sweeping "cellular renewal" language used to market it. A solid moisturizer; the specific TFC8 breakthrough claim rests on brand-commissioned data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TFC8 in Augustinus Bader The Cream?

TFC8 (Trigger Factor Complex) is the brand's proprietary name for a blend of vitamins, lipids, and peptides layered into the formula. It is not a single disclosed ingredient on the INCI list, so its exact composition and concentration are not independently verifiable.

Does Augustinus Bader The Cream have real clinical studies?

The brand cites a 4-week clinical trial with 20 participants for anti-wrinkle claims and a 4-week consumer perception study with 52 participants. These are real but small-scale, brand-commissioned studies rather than large independent trials.

Is Augustinus Bader The Cream worth the price?

The disclosed active ingredients — peptides like Palmitoyl Tripeptide-8, ceramides, alanyl glutamine, and antioxidant vitamin forms — are individually well-evidenced for hydration and barrier support. Whether the undisclosed TFC8 complex justifies the premium price over comparable peptide-and-ceramide moisturizers is not independently verifiable.
