ABOUT DOUBLE CHECK

We read the label so you don't have to.

Double Check exists because most beauty marketing claims sound scientific without being checked. We check them.

Why This Exists

Skincare and makeup packaging is full of confident language — "clinically proven," "10x stronger," "cellular regeneration" — that most shoppers have no easy way to verify. The gap between what a label implies and what the actual ingredient list supports is often wide, and it's rarely in the brand's interest to close that gap for you.

Double Check reads the full ingredient list on every product we cover, checks each headline claim against what's actually formulated and what independent evidence exists, and publishes a plain verdict. Not a takedown, not an endorsement — just what holds up and what doesn't.

How We Verify Each Case

  1. Read the full ingredient list

    We pull the complete INCI ingredient list for the product, not just the two or three actives the brand highlights on the front of the bottle.

  2. Identify the headline claim

    We note the specific marketing claim being made — a percentage, a named "complex," a comparative statement like "10x stronger."

  3. Check each ingredient against available evidence

    Every notable ingredient gets checked against what's independently published about it — not just what the brand or its own commissioned studies say.

  4. Stamp a verdict

    Each case closes with one of three verdicts, explained below, plus the specific reasoning behind it.

What Our Verdicts Mean

VERIFIED

The core claim holds up reasonably well against the ingredient list and available evidence.

MIXED

Some claims check out, others are plausible but not independently proven at the scale marketed.

FLAGGED

The claim runs meaningfully ahead of what the ingredient list or evidence actually supports.

A "verified" stamp means the core claim holds up — not that every word on the label is literally true down to the last adjective. Each case file explains the specific reasoning.

Makeup Works Differently

Our Makeup section uses a lighter screen than our full skincare case files — checking for common allergens, comedogenic red flags, and formulation transparency rather than a full ingredient-by-ingredient breakdown. It's built for browsing safe picks quickly, not as a substitute for checking your own sensitivities.

How We're Funded

Double Check is reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate or product links, and purchases made through them may help fund the site. Funding never changes a verdict — a product doesn't get a better stamp because we link to it. Full disclosure is in our Terms & Privacy page.

Questions or Corrections

Spot something inaccurate in a case file, or think we got a verdict wrong? We want to know. Ingredient science and product formulations change, and we'd rather correct a case file than leave it wrong.
