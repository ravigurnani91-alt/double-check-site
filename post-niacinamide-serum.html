Who should skip it

If you're dealing with active cystic acne, this alone won't be enough — you'll want something with salicylic acid or a dermatologist-guided routine alongside it. And if your skin is already very dry, pair it with a heavier moisturizer, since niacinamide on its own can feel a little thin in colder months.
