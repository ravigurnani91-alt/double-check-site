Why This Is Different From a Typical Beauty Placement

Most beauty coverage is either overtly sponsored or vaguely "gifted" content with no real scrutiny. Readers have gotten good at spotting both, and increasingly ignore them.

Double Check runs every product — partnered or not — through the same process: the marketing claim, checked against the actual ingredient list and available evidence, stamped VERIFIED, MIXED, or FLAGGED. We don't change a verdict for a partner. That's the entire point, and it's exactly what makes a good verdict worth something.

Ways to Work With Us

  • Standard Case File

    We independently review your product using our normal process. No payment required, no guaranteed outcome — but a strong ingredient story tends to earn a strong verdict.

  • Early Access Review

    Send us a product ahead of a public launch. We verify it and publish alongside your release, giving customers a trust signal from day one instead of after the fact.

  • Sponsored Deep-Dive (Clearly Labeled)

    A longer-form, brand-commissioned feature exploring your formulation philosophy in depth. Always disclosed as sponsored — we don't blur that line, because the disclosure is what keeps every other case file credible.

  • Ingredient Checker Integration

    Get your full ingredient list added to our free public Ingredient Checker tool, so anyone can verify your product against our database directly.

What we won't do: change a verdict for payment, remove a flagged claim because a brand asks, or publish a review without actually reading the ingredient list. If that's not the kind of coverage you're looking for, we're probably not the right fit — and that's fine.
