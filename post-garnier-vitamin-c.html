CASE 0001 — SERUMS / BRIGHTENING

Garnier Vitamin C + Face Serum

7 min read·Updated this month
CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
"100X stronger than Vitamin C — dark spots fade in 3 days"

This is one of the most-bought serums in India right now, sitting at the top of Myntra's personal care listings with over 40,000 ratings behind it. So before deciding whether the claim above holds up, here's what's actually in the bottle.

The evidence

Ingredient lists are ordered by concentration — what's near the top is doing the most work. Here's the breakdown, in order.

IngredientWhat it actually does
NiacinamideThe real star — supports skin barrier, calms inflammation, genuinely helps even tone
3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic AcidA stable, gentle Vitamin C derivative — less irritating, but also less potent than pure Vitamin C
Salicylic Acid (BHA)Mild exfoliant, present at a low, gentle concentration
Sodium HyaluronateHydration — binds water to the skin
AdenosineSoothing, barrier-repairing

The "100X stronger" line isn't a measurable claim — it's not clear what it's being compared against. What's actually here is a well-balanced, low-irritation brightening formula built around niacinamide, not a high-strength Vitamin C treatment. If pure, potent Vitamin C is specifically what you want, this isn't that. If you want a gentle daily brightener that won't sting, that's exactly what this is.

VERIFIED*

The core promise — visible brightening with low irritation — holds up. The "100X" figure specifically does not, since it's unverifiable marketing language rather than a real measurement.

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

  • Genuinely gentle — low irritation risk for sensitive skin
  • Niacinamide + BHA is a solid, evidence-backed pairing
  • Lightweight texture, layers easily
  • Very accessible price for daily use

RED FLAGS

  • "100X" and "3 days" claims aren't verifiable
  • Vitamin C concentration is mild, not for strong-actives routines
  • Contains fragrance (linalool, geraniol) — note if you react to scent

Who should actually buy this

Good fit if you want a low-effort, low-irritation entry into brightening actives — especially if you're new to niacinamide or Vitamin C. Less of a fit if you're already using stronger actives and want something to push results further, rather than maintain them.
