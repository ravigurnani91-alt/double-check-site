We Verified 20 Skincare Products' Marketing Claims — Here's Who Actually Won

Roundup · All Categories · 9 min read
By Double Check Team

You've probably wondered if the ₹15,000 cream actually works better than the ₹400 one, or if it's just packaging and a name. We checked the ingredient list on 20 real products — from Garnier to La Mer — against what they actually claim. Here's what held up.

The Surprise: Price Didn't Predict the Verdict

Two of our only fully "VERIFIED" case files were Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair and Tatcha's Dewy Skin Cream — both luxury, but for genuinely evidenced reasons, not just brand prestige. Meanwhile, several products well over ₹10,000 landed as "MIXED," with their headline "proprietary complex" claims resting on thinner evidence than a ₹200 kajal's safety claim.

The Real Takeaway

Price buys you formulation philosophy, packaging, and brand story — not automatically better evidence. Some of the most convincing "proprietary technology" names we checked were built on ingredients with real but modest research, dressed up in language that implies far more than what's independently proven.
